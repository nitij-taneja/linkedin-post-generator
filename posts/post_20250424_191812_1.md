Discriminative vs. Generative Models

Curated by Avi Chawla and Akshay Pachaar from Daily Dose of Data Science:

• Discriminative models predict the probability of a sample belonging to a class, given the input features. Examples: Logistic Regression, Support Vector Machines (SVMs).

• Generative models generate new samples that resemble the training data. Examples: Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), Variational Autoencoders (VAEs).
