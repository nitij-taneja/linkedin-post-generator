📹💡 Evaluating Complex Video Reasoning with MINERVA! 🚀

Current video benchmarks only provide outcome supervision, making it hard to assess if models truly combine perceptual and temporal info to reason about videos. To fix this, MINERVA provides a new dataset with:

✨ Detailed, hand-crafted reasoning traces for each question
✨odal, diverse video domains and lengths
✨ Complex multi-step questions

Benchmarking shows MINERVA is a challenge for frontier models! Error analysis reveals common failure modes: temporal localization and visual perception errors. 🤔

Impact: Improved video reasoning models for applications like video summarization, question answering, and autonomous systems.

Next: How can we leverage human and LLM-as-a-judge methods for scoring video reasoning traces? 🤝
