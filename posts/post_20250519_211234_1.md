🚀 Advance in AI Training with HelpSteer3-Preference Dataset! 🚀

In a significant leap forward, researchers have introduced HelpSteer3-Preference, a massive dataset of over 40,000 human-annotated samples, spanning diverse real-world applications of large language models (LLMs), including STEM, coding, and multilingual scenarios.

What makes it matter? This dataset is crucial for training general-domain, instruction-following language models with Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF). The better the dataset, the better the AI model.

The researchers achieved top performance on RM-Bench (82.4%) and JudgeBench (73.7%), a substantial 10% absolute improvement over previous results.

Key term: RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)

The dataset is available under a permissive license (CC-BY-4.0): https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/HelpSteer3#preference

Read the full paper: http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11475v1

Authors: Zhilin Wang, Jiaqi Zeng, Olivier Delalleau et al.
