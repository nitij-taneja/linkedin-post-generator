## Content

🔍️ Ever wondered how to evaluate taxonomies without a "gold standard"? 🤔 Researchers from Pascal Wullschleger, Majid Zarharan, Donnacha Daly et al. have introduced two innovative metrics to do just that! 🎉

The first metric measures robustness by correlating semantic and taxonomic similarity, covering errors that existing metrics miss. Think of it like checking if a taxonomy's logic is sound, without needing a perfect reference.

The second metric uses Natural Language Inference (NLI) to assess logical adequacy. Imagine NLI as a reasoning framework that helps evaluate whether a taxonomy makes sense.

Both metrics were tested on five taxonomies and showed strong correlation with F1 scores against gold-standard taxonomies.
