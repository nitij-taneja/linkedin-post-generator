## Content

🤔 Ever wondered how AI models process multiple images to answer complex queries? 📸🤯

New research by Aaryan Sharma, Shivansh Gupta, Samar Agarwal, and others tackles this challenge by introducing submodular subset selection techniques in Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) 🚀. The goal? Enhance Multi-Image Question Answering scenarios, where scalability and performance issues arise with increasing image numbers. 📈

Here's the key insight: By leveraging query-aware submodular functions like GraphCut, the retriever framework can pre-select a subset of semantically relevant images before the main retrieval component kicks in 🔓. Think of it as a filtering process that prioritizes the most relevant images, making the entire process more efficient 🕒.

The researchers demonstrate that using anchor-based queries and augmenting the data improves effectiveness, especially with large haystacks 🌳. In essence, this approach enables AI models to better through complex visual data and provide more reliable answers 🔝.
