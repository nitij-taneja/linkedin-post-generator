🚀 Reinforcement Learning Boosts Data Assimilation! 🌎

Meet RL-DAUNCE, a new method that leverages reinforcement learning (RL) to enhance data assimilation with physical constraints. 💡 Key ideas:
- RL-DAUNCE efficiency while structuring agents like ensemble members
- Emphasize uncertainty quantification with multiple ensemble members
- Enforce physical constraints during assimilation with a primal-dual optimization strategy

Impact and use cases:
- Improve state estimation and forecasting in complex systems (e.g., atmospheric phenomena like Madden-Julian Oscillation)
- Outperform standard ensemble Kalman filter (EnKF) in constrained environments
