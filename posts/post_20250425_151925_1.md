🤖💡 Unlocking the power of sparse attention in Transformer LLMs! 🚀

Researchers explore the trade-offs of sparse attention in processing long sequences, revealing key insights:

✨ Larger, highly sparse models outperform smaller, dense ones for very long sequences.
✨ Sparsity levels can be higher during decoding than prefilling, and correlate with model size.
✨ No one-size-fits-all strategy; different tasks and phases require unique sparsification approaches.
✨ Moderate sparsity can lead to significant performance degradation in some tasks.

These findings have significant implications for enhancing Transformer LLM capabilities, but require careful evaluation of trade-offs for performance-sensitive applications.
