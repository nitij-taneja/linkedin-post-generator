🚀 Preventing Robot Failures in Real-Time: A Breakthrough in AI Reasoning

Imagine a scenario where a robot encounters an unexpected situation beyond its training data, and it needs to prevent a failure in real-time. Current methods rely on pre-defined intervention policies, but this approach lacks flexibility and safety guarantees. To address this challenge, researchers have developed FORTRESS, a framework that generates and reasons about semantically safe fallback strategies in real-time.

FORTRESS uses multi-modal reasoners to identify goals and anticipate failure modes at a low frequency during nominal operations. When a fallback response is triggered, it rapidly synthesizes plans while inferring and avoiding semantically unsafe regions in real-time.

The key innovation lies in bridging open-world, multi-modal reasoning with dynamics-aware planning, eliminating the need for hard-coded fallbacks and human safety interventions. FORTRESS outperforms existing methods in safety classification accuracy and improves system safety and planning success in simulation and real-world robot data.
