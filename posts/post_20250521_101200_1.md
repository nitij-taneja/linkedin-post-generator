## Content

🔍 Protecting AI-generated images from misuse just got a whole lot easier! 💡

Researchers have created a watermarking framework, IndexMark, that safeguards autoregressive image generation models from being used maliciously. 🔒 What's the magic? By replacing generated indices with similar ones, the watermark is embedded without affecting image quality! 📸

Here's the intuitive insight: think of the codebook as a library where each book has a unique index. IndexMark carefully selects watermark "tokens" from the codebook, based on token similarity, and replaces them with the watermark, promoting its use without altering the image. 🔗

The verification process involves calculating the proportion of watermark tokens in generated images, making it robust against various perturbations like cropping, noise, and JPEG compression. 📊

What does this mean? It's a major step forward in protecting image ownership and preventing misuse! 🚫
