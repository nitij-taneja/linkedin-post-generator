"Tiny but Mighty: Revolutionizing AI on Microcontrollers! 🤖

Imagine AI models that fit on microcontrollers with just 128kB of RAM, yet still deliver real-time results! 🕒️ Researchers have been working on patch-based fusion to optimize data flows across neural network layers. Now, a new technique called msf-CNN takes it to the next level.

msf-CNN efficiently finds the best fusion settings for Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) by navigating a directed acyclic graph. This means it can achieve inference using 50% less RAM compared to previous work! 💻

The implementation is available for various microcontrollers (ARM Cortex-M, RISC-V, ESP32). This breakthrough offers system designers more flexibility and opens up new possibilities for edge computing.
