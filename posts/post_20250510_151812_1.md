🤖💡 "Revolutionizing Business Process Management with Conversational AI" 

Imagine redesigning process models with ease, using natural language inputs and receiving explainable, reproducible changes. This research introduces a Conversational Process Model Redesign (CPD) approach, leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs) to empower domain experts. 

CPD receives input, identifies change patterns from literature, rephrases the change request, and applies the change to the process model. The results show that LLM-based approach can handle most changes well, but users need support to describe changes clearly. 

Impact: AI-augmented Business Process Management systems to improve process model design and redesign. Use cases: streamlining operations, enhancing customer experience, and increasing efficiency.

What's next? How can we further improve user support for describing changes? Can we integrate CPD into existing BPM systems? 🤔💡
