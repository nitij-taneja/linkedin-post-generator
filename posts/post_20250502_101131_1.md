🤖 Rethinking Memory in AI: A New Framework 🤖

Memory is a fundamental component of AI systems, particularly in large language models (LLMs). While previous surveys focused on memory applications in LLMs, they often overlooked the atomic operations that drive memory dynamics.

This survey categorizes representations into parametric, contextual structured, and contextual unstructured, and introduces 6 fundamental memory operations: Consolidation, Updating, Indexing, Forgetting, Retrieval, and Compression.

Impact: This framework provides a structured and dynamic perspective on research, datasets, and tools related to memory in AI, clarifying the functional interplay in LLMs-based agents.

Use cases: Long-term, long-context, parametric modification, and multi-source memory.

Questions: What are the implications of this framework on the future of AI research and applications? How can we leverage this framework to develop more efficient and effective AI systems?
